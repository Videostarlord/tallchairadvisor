# Strategic Thesis

**Last revised:** 2026-04-06

## The Bet

TallChairAdvisor.com can become the #1 resource for height-specific office chair ergonomics by owning a sub-niche that no competitor explicitly targets: "does this chair fit someone who is 6'X?"

**Why it works:**
1. Underserved demand — real search volume, few authoritative answers
2. High purchase intent — $700–$2,000 chairs, careful research
3. Spec-driven content — verifiable numbers resist AI displacement
4. No incumbent — BTOD does weight, Tall.Life does lifestyle, nobody does height-specific chair fit

## Current State (Apr 6)

- **Site age:** ~100 days
- **SEO score:** 89/100
- **GSC:** ~4,100 impressions, 12 clicks, 0.29% CTR
- **Pages:** ~37 content pages, all now indexed
- **Revenue:** $0 (no clicks converting yet)

## What Needs to Happen Next (Priority Order)

### 1. Fix CTR (immediate)
The site gets impressions but not clicks. Three pages at pos 7–10 with 0% CTR are the biggest leak. Verdict-first meta descriptions are the prescribed fix. This is unproven — track before/after.

### 2. Ranking Lift on Flagship (/review/gesture/)
Position 10.31 is the threshold between "gets clicks" and "doesn't." Internal link authority from high-impression pages needs to push this to pos 6–8. This is the single highest-leverage ranking improvement.

### 3. Content Differentiation (AI-era moat)
The height-bracket verdict table on /best-office-chairs/ and citation capsules site-wide are the AI-era content moat. No competitor has structured height-specific data in a format AI systems can cite. This is TCA's long-term defensibility.

### 4. Standing Desk Content (zero competition)
Confirmed demand signal. Jackson has a real desk. ME background. Zero competition. This is the next content pillar.

### 5. Depth Upgrades on Thin Pages
/office-chairs-for-tall-people/ is buried at pos 69 for core "best office chair for tall people" queries. Needs major content depth upgrade. Height guide pages (6-foot-5/6/7) scoring 67–68 need similar treatment.

## What Could Change This Thesis

- **If CTR fixes don't work:** The 0% CTR might be caused by Google answering in SERPs (AI Overviews), not bad meta. In that case, pivot to citation optimization over CTR optimization.
- **If a major competitor enters the height-specific niche:** First-mover advantage has a time limit. Speed of content publication matters.
- **If affiliate revenue never materializes:** The "right" content may need to shift toward more transactional formats (direct product links, pricing, deals).
