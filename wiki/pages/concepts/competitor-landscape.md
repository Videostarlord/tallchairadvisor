# Competitor Landscape

## Direct Competitors

### BTOD.com (DA 45–55)
- **Focus:** Big AND tall chairs (weight-focused)
- **Strength:** 15+ years, authorized dealer, deep content, video, strong E-E-A-T
- **TCA gap to exploit:** BTOD doesn't address 6'4" seat height needs specifically. They frame everything around weight capacity, not height.
- **Keywords they own:** "best big and tall office chair", "steelcase leap plus review"

### ChairsFX.com (DA 20–30)
- **Focus:** Gaming + ergonomic comparisons
- **Strength:** Good comparison tables, fast site
- **TCA gap to exploit:** Generic comparisons — no "Verdict for Tall People" sections
- **Keywords they own:** "steelcase gesture vs herman miller aeron"

### Tall.Life (DA 15–25)
- **Focus:** Tall lifestyle (clothing, chairs, beds, cars)
- **Strength:** Niche alignment with tall audience
- **TCA gap to exploit:** Shallow chair content. Listicles, not deep reviews. TCA should outrank within 3–6 months on chair queries.
- **Keywords they own:** "best office chairs for tall people"

### StandingDeskTopper.com (DA 25–35)
- **Focus:** Standing desks primarily
- **TCA gap to exploit:** Standing desk height guide for tall people. Future content opportunity.

### TheHumanSolution.com (DA 35–45)
- **Focus:** Authorized retailer + educational blog
- **TCA gap to exploit:** General ergonomics advice — TCA wins on height-specific how-to

## TCA Competitive Advantages

1. **Height specificity** — nobody answers "Is this chair good for 6'3"?" with actual math
2. **Spec accuracy** — precise seat height, depth, back height with context for tall users
3. **Automation potential** — Astro allows programmatic micro-pages at scale
4. **Content freshness** — 2026 reviews while competitors have stale 2022 lists
5. **Clean architecture** — hub-and-spoke built from day one

## Format Gaps (TCA vs Competitors)

Content formats competitors use that TCA is missing or partially has:
- ✅ Comparison tables (added to aeron/tall-people)
- ✅ FAQ sections (on reviews/comparisons)
- ✅ Quick Answer / TL;DR boxes
- ✅ Author bio + byline
- ❌ Height-specific fit chart / visual — **no competitor has this either = differentiator**
- ❌ Video embeds (YouTube)
- ❌ Star rating display
