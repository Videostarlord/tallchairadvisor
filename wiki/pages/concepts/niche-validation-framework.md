# Niche Validation Framework

## Core Principle

Do not use early revenue to decide whether a young SEO niche is alive. Separate:
- **Demand validation** — do people ask the question?
- **Distribution validation** — will Google match and surface the content?
- **Monetization validation** — can the traffic convert into meaningful revenue?

The common failure is mixing these into one test and calling the niche "dead" too early.

## TCA-Specific Conclusion

**TCA is not dead.** As of 2026-05-11, the site has **15,417 impressions**, **35 clicks**, **avg position 11**, and a first affiliate commission. That proves:
- demand exists
- Google is matching the site to real queries
- the site can rank on a young domain

The current problem is **CTR and monetization efficiency**, not niche existence.

## Recommended Validation Stack

### 1. Pre-build scorecard
Before building, score:
- demand density
- SERP penetrability
- format advantage
- monetization depth
- adjacency potential

If the total is weak, do not build.

### 2. SEO MVP
Publish a small cluster:
- 1 pillar
- 3 commercial pages
- 5 informational pages
- 1 comparison or best page

Read:
- indexation
- impressions
- query matching
- whether any cluster reaches roughly positions 15-40

### 3. Paid validation
Use a small ad test only when economics are unclear. This validates demand and landing-page conversion faster than SEO, but it does **not** validate organic difficulty.

## Kill Criteria

Kill or pause when:
- query matching never materializes
- impressions stay flat as indexed pages rise
- no page cluster breaks toward page 2-4 after a real content set is live
- monetization is structurally weak even if traffic exists

Do **not** kill a niche just because a fresh domain has weak clicks at day 30-60.

## Expansion Rule

For TCA, the fastest expansion is not more fresh domains. It is adjacent revenue streams around the same audience:
- standing desks
- monitor arms / monitor height
- footrests
- seat cushions
- return-policy / purchase-anxiety content
- long-legs / 6'4" buying pages

This preserves:
- topical trust
- internal linking
- existing ranking momentum
- the same affiliate visitor path
