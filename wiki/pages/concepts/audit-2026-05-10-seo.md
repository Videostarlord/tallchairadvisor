## Top New Findings (not previously tracked)

### Critical — Schema
- **`itemReviewed` missing from all 4 Review schema nodes** — most likely reason review rich results aren't appearing in SERPs. Fix: add `"itemReviewed": { "@id": "...#product/[chair]" }` to Review node on gesture, aeron-size-c, leap-plus, sihoo-doro-s300.
- **WebSite schema missing `@id`** — WebPage node references `#website` via `isPartOf` but WebSite has no `@id`. Dangling reference. Fix: add `"@id": "https://tallchairadvisor.com/#website"` to WebSite node in index.astro.
- **`/chairs/steelcase-gesture/index.astro`** — missing `datePublished`, `dateModified`, and author `@id`. Only page that cannot qualify for Article rich results.
- **Product `@id` missing on Aeron and Leap Plus reviews** — only Gesture has a Product `@id`. Cross-page comparison references can't resolve to canonical entities.
- **Gesture seat depth spec error in knee-pain FAQ**: "17.75 inches" → should be "18.75 inches".
- **Article `@id` missing site-wide** on all 6 Article-typed pages.
- **Publisher logo is OG image**, not a dedicated logo asset.

### Critical — Compliance
- **Affiliate disclosure absent from body on 6 pages**: Homepage, Knee Pain, Shoulder Pain, Chair Dimensions, Pillar, Standing Desk. FTC requires "clear and conspicuous" — footer link only doesn't meet this standard.

### High — Conversion
- **Aeron vs Gesture: 0 affiliate links in first 84% of page** — 348 impressions/month, 0 clicks. Both Amazon links at 85-86% into HTML.
- **Aeron vs Gesture: H1/title mismatch** — title says "Why I Chose the Gesture" (personal), H1 says "Herman Miller Aeron Size C vs Steelcase Gesture" (generic). Searchers clicking the personal-angle title see a generic header. Explains the CTR problem alongside meta issues.
- **Aeron vs Gesture: Quick Answer is non-verdict** — "Aeron for breathability; Gesture for armrests" doesn't deliver on "Why I Chose" promise.
- **Gesture Review: single affiliate link at 85% of page** — add CTA after DIRECT ANSWER box.
- **Best Office Chairs: Quick Picks links go to internal pages, not Amazon**.

### Medium — Technical
- **5 height landing pages have zero images** — no LCP image candidate, no image search traffic.
- **Image Cache-Control conflict**: `max-age=3600, immutable, must-revalidate` — `immutable` and `must-revalidate` contradict. Fix in `public/_headers`.
- **`/standing-desk-height-tall-people/`** has only 1 inbound link — near-orphaned despite priority 0.8 in sitemap.
- **Stale sitemap lastmod**: /review/gesture/ at 2026-03-07, /office-chairs-for-tall-people/ at 2026-03-08; /chairs/herman-miller-aeron/size-guide/ has no lastmod at all.
- **4 meta descriptions over 160 chars**: aeron-size-c (166), leap-plus (170), gesture-vs-leap-plus (165), standing-desk (161).
- **Deprecated `priority` + `changefreq`** on all 40 sitemap URLs — ignored by Google since 2019.
