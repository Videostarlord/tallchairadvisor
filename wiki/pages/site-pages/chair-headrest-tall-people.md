## Notable

**Only the Aeron has buyable aftermarket headrests.** Three honesty positions built into the page: (1) the Steelcase Gesture has NO aftermarket headrest — Steelcase bundles it only at chair purchase; (2) the Atlas Leap V2 headrest (B0GTJ3YT1B) is a real product but sold out at Atlas direct, Crandall, and Office Logix as of 2026-08-04, and its Amazon listing does not resolve — the page links Atlas unaffiliated for stock-tracking rather than shipping a dead CTA; (3) it does NOT fit the Leap Plus or Leap V1, confirmed by both Atlas and Crandall. That last point matters commercially: the Leap Plus is the chair most often recommended to 6'2"+ buyers and has no retrofit path to head support at all.

Weakest of the five on product availability, strongest on differentiation — no competitor states the Gesture and Leap Plus gaps.
