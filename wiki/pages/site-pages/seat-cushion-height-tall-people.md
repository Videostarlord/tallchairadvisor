## Notable

Corrects a common search intent competitors get wrong: **chair leg extenders do not work on gas-cylinder office chairs** (they are for four-legged furniture). Information gain is the trade-off analysis — a cushion raises seat height but costs backrest height and pushes the lumbar curve out of position, which matters more for tall users. Design rule stated: max the cylinder first, then use the thinnest cushion that closes the gap.
