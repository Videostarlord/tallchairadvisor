## Remaining Issues

### High

#### 1. Inconsistent Author @id in Schema
Review pages use two different author identifiers:
- `/review/aeron-size-c/` and `/review/leap-plus/`: `https://tallchairadvisor.com/about/#author`
- `/review/gesture/`: `https://tallchairadvisor.com/author/jackson-christopher/#person`

**Fix:** Standardize all review/article schemas to use one @id. Recommend: `https://tallchairadvisor.com/author/jackson-christopher/#person` (matches the dedicated author page URL).

---

### Medium

#### 2. /review/gesture/ Meta Description 175 Chars
**Current:** "Evaluated at 6'4" — the Gesture's 18-21 in seat height, 20.5 in seat depth, 24 in armrest width, and 330 lb limit. Full adjustment range and user experience review."
**Length:** 175 characters (limit: 160)
**Fix:** Trim to ≤160 chars. Example: "Evaluated at 6'4" — the Gesture's seat height, seat depth, armrest width, and 330 lb limit reviewed. Full adjustment range and user experience."

#### 3. Chair Subpages with Low Inlinks (Orphan Risk)
These chair-specific subpages receive very few inlinks from other pages:

| Page | Inlinks | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| /chairs/herman-miller-aeron/tall-people/ | 3 | High orphan risk |
| /chairs/steelcase-gesture/weight-limit/ | 3 | High orphan risk |
| /chairs/herman-miller-aeron/seat-height/ | 4 | Medium orphan risk |
| /chairs/steelcase-leap-plus/seat-height/ | 4 | Medium orphan risk |
| /chairs/steelcase-gesture/tall-people/ | 5 | Medium orphan risk |
| /chairs/steelcase-leap-plus/tall-people/ | 5 | Medium orphan risk |

**Fix:** Add contextual links to these pages from high-inlink hub pages: `/best-office-chairs/`, `/office-chairs-for-tall-people/`, `/correct-chair-dimensions/`, `/fit-guides/`, and comparison pages.

#### 4. Missing `wordCount` on All 20 Article Schemas
All Article-typed JSON-LD blocks lack the `wordCount` property. This is a Google-supported field for knowledge graph richness.
**Fix:** Add `"wordCount": N` to each Article schema at build time.

### Low

#### 5. /chairs/steelcase-leap-plus/ Thin Content
- **Word count:** 908 (lowest among non-utility pages)
- **H2s:** 4, **H3s:** 4

#### 6. Missing BreadcrumbList on /about/

#### 8. No JSON-LD on Utility Pages

**Fix:** Add minimal `WebPage` schema to each.
