## 4. High Priority Issues

### 4a. LCP Images Missing fetchpriority
**Confirmed unfixed.**

**Homepage (/):**
```html
<img src="/images/jackson-christopher.webp" ... loading="lazy">
```
No preload link. No fetchpriority. The jackson-christopher.webp is the first (and only) above-fold image — this is the LCP candidate. It is loading lazy, which defers it unnecessarily.

**Fix:** Pass `preloadImage="/images/jackson-christopher.webp"` prop to Layout on homepage. Set `loading="eager"` and `fetchpriority="high"` on the img tag.

**Best Office Chairs (/best-office-chairs/):**
```html
<img src="/images/aeron-size-c-hero.webp" ... loading="lazy">
<img src="/images/leap-plus-hero.webp" ... loading="lazy">
<img src="/images/gesture-hero.webp" ... loading="lazy">
```

Three product images above the fold, all loading lazy. First one (Aeron) is likely the LCP candidate. No preload links.

**Fix:** Add `fetchpriority="high"` and `loading="eager"` to the first product image. Pass it as `preloadImage` to Layout.

## 6. CTR Optimization Opportunities

### Priority 1: /chairs/herman-miller-aeron/tall-people/ (83 impr, pos 7.4, 0 clicks)
**MOST URGENT.** Position 7 with 83 impressions and zero clicks is a significant CTR problem. At pos 7, typical CTR should be ~2-3%. Getting 0% means the title/description isn't compelling searchers to click.

**Current:**
- Title (56): `Aeron Size C: Tall People Fit Guide | Tall Chair Advisor`
- Desc (159): `Aeron Size C fit analysis for tall users 6'0-6'6. Fixed seat depth trade-off explained, height-by-height breakdown, and who should choose Gesture or Leap Plus.`

**Problem:** The title is descriptive but generic. "Fit Guide" is not a click-triggering phrase. The description mentions competitors in a way that might make users think they should read the Gesture/Leap Plus pages instead.

**Suggested revision:**
- Title (≤60): `Herman Miller Aeron Size C: Tall People Fit Analysis`
- Desc (≤160): `At 6'0–6'6, the Aeron's fixed 18.25" seat depth is the key trade-off. Height-by-height verdict: who it fits, who should choose the Leap Plus instead.`

The description should end with a reason to click — the promise of the height-specific verdict. Avoid mentioning competitor names in the meta description as it may create decision paralysis.

### Priority 2: /chairs/steelcase-gesture/seat-depth/ (85 impr, pos 10.06, 0 clicks)
This page is also at ~pos 10 with high impressions and zero clicks. The query "steelcase gesture seat depth range inches" is at pos 5.57 — very close to page 1.

**Current:**
- Title (63): `Gesture Seat Depth: Is 18.75" Enough? | Tall Chair Advisor` ← OVER 60 CHARS
- Desc (160): `Adjusts 15.75"–18.75": adequate for most tall users to 6'3", borderline at 6'4". Full spec breakdown with by-height comparison to the Leap Plus.`

**Problem:** Title is 63 chars (gets truncated in SERP). "Is 18.75" Enough?" is a good question-format hook but the title is too long.

**Suggested revision:**
- Title (≤60): `Steelcase Gesture Seat Depth Range: 15.75"–18.75"`
- Desc (≤160): `Gesture seat depth adjusts 15.75"–18.75". Adequate for tall users through 6'3", borderline at 6'4". How to set it and how it compares to the Leap Plus.`

Including the actual range numbers in the title directly answers the informational query and increases CTR.

### Priority 3: /review/gesture/ (443 combined impr, pos ~10-13, 2 clicks)
Query "steelcase gesture review independent" at pos 8.91 with 0 clicks suggests users are seeking reviews they can trust. The word "independent" in the query is a trust signal.

**Current:**
- Title (55): `Steelcase Gesture Review (2026): Tall User Fit Analysis`
- Desc (155): `Evaluated at 6'4" — the Gesture's 18.75" seat depth fits most tall users through 6'3". Height-by-height verdict with armrest and back analysis.`

**Suggestion:** The title is solid. The desc could be strengthened by emphasizing first-person ownership/testing:
- Desc (≤160): `Daily-use review by a 6'4" ME student who owns the Gesture. Seat depth, armrests, back height verdict by height — who it fits and who should look at the Leap Plus.`

### Priority 4: /chairs/steelcase-leap-plus/seat-height/ (42 impr, pos 10.95, 1 click)
Query "steelcase leap seat height range 15.5 20.5" at pos 9.17 — the exact spec is in the query.

**Current:**
- Title (65): `Leap Plus Seat Height: Is 22.5" Enough? | Tall Chair Advisor` ← OVER 60 CHARS
